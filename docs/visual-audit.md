# Visual audit

The public Volta recording and the implementation desktop recording were viewed at 1440×900. The opening split typography, two rows of eleven studies, a central services wheel, dark work chapter, indigo extruded signature, horizontal process, arced studio cards and dark offers reflect the visible composition. Intentional differences are the Out There wordmark, original abstract studies and actual project content.

Corrections made after inspection include the work card sticky position, gallery card styles, process and offers palette, typography contrast and the mobile menu. The service wheel still uses CSS 3D positions and the work cards use original CSS art rather than the reference imagery. Exact camera and shader parameters remain unavailable from public preview footage.

Screenshots are under artifacts/visual-validation. Both full walkthrough recordings are in references/volta and artifacts/visual-validation.
