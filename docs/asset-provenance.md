# Asset provenance

- The Out There mark and wordmarks were copied from the user-owned Training Ground project.
- Eleven hero studies and eight sculptural studies were procedurally rendered by the project scripts under tools/. The final encoded WebP files are under dist/assets/.
- The extruded wordmark mesh was created from Three.js Helvetiker geometry by tools/hero/build-lettering.mjs. Its material pattern and deformation are written in dist/wordmark.js.
- Inter and DM Mono are bundled locally from their open-source font packages.
- GSAP, Three.js and Lenis are bundled locally from the installed npm packages.
- Volta recordings under references/volta/ are internal visual references and are outside the served dist directory.
