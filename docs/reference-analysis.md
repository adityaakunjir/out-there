# Volta Atelier reference study

The desktop reference was recorded at 1440 × 900. The complete 118-second scroll and 27-second pointer study are in references/volta. overview.jpg samples the first recording every eight seconds. The public preview and the public catalogue descriptions informed the reconstruction. The imagery, wording and geometry in the implementation are independently made for Out There.

| Chapter | Visible in the full-page recording | Reconstruction decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Two offset rows of narrow portraits framed by huge opposite-corner words. Fine metadata, fixed navigation and very little chrome. | Two-row editorial studies, split Out There lettering and the Training Ground mark. Keep the menu and enquiry outside the pointer-reactive layer. |
| Services | Central red wheel with a bright active line. Four proof tiles sit below it. | Seven scroll-selected capabilities. Replace sales figures with factual scope. |
| Work | Single central composition with ghosted neighbours. The active index stays near the lower left while work moves through the centre. | Fanned project covers, an index rail and meaningful project details. |
| Signature | An indigo patterned solid wordmark fills a dark stage. Its silhouette and visible sides change with scroll. | Original extruded Out There lettering with reversible deformation. |
| Process | White headline on charcoal. Dark rectangular cards enter from the right. | Five stages on a measured horizontal track. |
| Studio | Centred statement followed by a shallow arc of cards on black. | Existing disciplines take the place of personal profiles. |
| Portrait cylinder | A second spatial arrangement of images follows the studio. | Showcase five original visual studies. |
| Offers / FAQ | Three dark offer columns, high-contrast primary action, FAQ text at left and divided rows at right. | Keep the existing enquiry terms, three paths and native disclosure semantics. |
| Close | Ghosted repeated typography, a dark footer and a dividing strip. | Local pointer response and an email link with a stable hit area. |

Camera lenses, shader parameters, scroll durations and easing curves cannot be inferred exactly from a recording. Values in the implementation are calibrated independently. Desktop reference: https://threeui.com/landing-pages/volta-atelier.html. Public component index: https://threeui.com/browse.
